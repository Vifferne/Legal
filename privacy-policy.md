# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 2026  
**Applies to:** Applications published by the Developer, to the extent the features described below are used in each application.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how our applications may access, use, transmit, store, and share information. Not every application uses every feature described here. The actual data processing depends on the application, its enabled features, and the permissions you grant.

**Publisher:** The Developer responsible for the application.

## 2. Devices, platforms and technical information

Our applications may run on smartphones, tablets, desktop or laptop computers, web browsers, Progressive Web Apps (PWAs), embedded WebViews, Android Auto, Android Automotive OS (AAOS), vehicle infotainment systems, and other compatible devices. Availability of features and permissions varies by device, platform, and application.

Where needed to provide a feature, an application or its service providers may process technical information such as device type, operating system and version, application or browser version, language, screen characteristics, network connectivity, IP address, and diagnostic or error information. Device sensors and capabilities (for example GPS, orientation, motion, or microphone input) are accessed only where implemented and permitted. Use of a capability does not necessarily mean that its data is stored or sent to a server.

## 3. Location and GPS data

Applications offering maps, navigation, nearby services, speed display, or location sharing may request access to the device's location, including precise GPS coordinates. Location may be used to display your position, calculate routes, provide navigation instructions, determine speed, or support features you explicitly activate.

Where an application offers voluntary location sharing, your location may be transmitted over the internet to a server and made available to the people or private groups with whom you choose to share it. Recipients may be able to view your current or recently shared location. The scope and duration of sharing depend on the application's controls and the selected connection or group.

Location access can be managed through your device settings. Disabling location permissions may prevent location-dependent features from working.

## 4. Vehicle data and in-car systems

Some applications may operate on Android Automotive OS (AAOS), Android Auto, or compatible in-car displays and infotainment systems. Depending on the application, vehicle manufacturer, operating system, permissions, and available interfaces, an application may access or receive vehicle-related information such as vehicle speed, driving or stationary state, or other vehicle signals made available through supported system APIs (including vehicle property interfaces where applicable).

Vehicle-related information may be used to display speed, adapt the user interface for driving, support navigation, or enable other clearly identified in-car functions. Availability varies by vehicle and application; this policy does not mean that every application accesses all such data.

Vehicle data processed solely on the device is not necessarily uploaded or retained. If a particular feature transmits vehicle-related data to a server or third-party service, that transmission must be disclosed accurately in the application's relevant notices. This policy does not imply access to vehicle identification numbers (VIN), diagnostics, or other vehicle identifiers unless a specific application actually accesses them.

## 5. Internet access and third-party services

Applications may use an internet connection to load maps, geocode addresses, search for places, calculate routes, retrieve traffic information, synchronize information, or enable sharing features. Depending on the application, requests may be sent to external map, routing, search, traffic, hosting, or infrastructure providers.

These providers may receive technical information required to process a request, potentially including IP address, requested coordinates, search terms, route endpoints, device or browser information, and request timestamps. Their handling of information is governed by their applicable privacy policies.

## 6. Information you provide

Depending on the application, you may voluntarily provide a display name, telephone number, saved places, routes, group names, invitation codes, or other information necessary for a feature. Where sharing is enabled, selected information may be visible to the participants you authorize.

## 7. Local storage and server storage

Applications may store settings, favorites, saved routes, or other preferences locally on your device. Features that require synchronization or sharing may store relevant information on a server for the time needed to provide that feature.

**Retention:** Retention depends on the feature and the information involved. Locally saved data may remain until you remove it or uninstall the application. Server-based information may remain while a connection or service is active and may be deleted or retained for a limited period afterward for operational, security, or legal reasons. Ending a connection stops the associated sharing, but does not necessarily erase all technical logs immediately.

## 8. Permissions

Depending on their functions, applications may request permissions such as location, background location, notifications, internet/network access, vehicle-related permissions or APIs where available, or other device capabilities. Permissions are requested only where relevant to the application's functions and subject to the device's permission controls.

## 9. Sharing of information

Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate the requested functions and, for voluntary sharing features, with the individuals or groups you select. The use of any additional analytics, advertising, or third-party services depends on the individual application and its installed components.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet transmission, device, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep private invitation links and codes confidential and share them only with intended recipients.

## 11. Your choices and rights

You can manage application permissions in your device settings and use in-app controls, where available, to stop location sharing, leave groups, or end connections. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data and to object to certain processing. Requests may be submitted through the support or contact channel made available for the relevant application.

**Data deletion:** Where available, use the application’s controls to end sharing or remove connections. For requests concerning personal data held by the Developer, use the support or contact channel made available for the relevant application. Any additional deletion procedure will be provided where applicable.

## 12. Children's privacy

The intended audience and any age restrictions depend on the individual application. Where an application is used by children, applicable privacy protections and parental-consent requirements must be respected. Location-sharing features should only be used with appropriate permission and supervision.

## 13. International processing

Some third-party services or hosting providers may process information outside your country. Where applicable, transfers are subject to relevant legal safeguards.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when application features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when revisions are published.

## 15. Contact

For privacy questions or personal-data requests, use the support or contact channel made available for the relevant application. No personal contact details are published in this document.
